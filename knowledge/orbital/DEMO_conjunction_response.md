# DEVELOPMENT/DEMO Orbital Conjunction Response

## Close approach assessment
DEVELOPMENT/DEMO MATERIAL - not real mission documentation. A conjunction assessment should consider miss distance, relative speed, time to closest approach, covariance quality, and the applicable mission safety threshold. A critical risk state requires confirmation against the authoritative tracking solution.

Operational guidance: review the latest conjunction inputs, confirm the miss distance and time to closest approach, and escalate according to approved flight-dynamics procedures when thresholds are exceeded.
