# DEVELOPMENT/DEMO Thermal and Telemetry Anomalies

## Correlated subsystem heating
DEVELOPMENT/DEMO MATERIAL - not real mission documentation. A thermal hotspot and a concurrent power, pressure, or temperature telemetry deviation may indicate a shared subsystem condition, but correlation alone does not prove causation. Check timestamps, sensor quality, and nearby subsystem measurements.

Operational guidance: inspect the thermal frame and sensor channels together, monitor the affected subsystem for persistence, and compare the event against recent operating mode changes.
