# DEVELOPMENT/DEMO Space Weather Effects

## Radiation and spacecraft systems
DEVELOPMENT/DEMO MATERIAL - not real mission documentation. Elevated radiation and geomagnetic conditions can increase spacecraft charging, single-event effects, and subsystem upset risk. Telemetry changes observed during the same interval should be correlated with the environmental record before assigning a mechanism.

Operational guidance: monitor radiation and power telemetry, compare event timing across systems, and review spacecraft charging indicators. Escalate to mission procedures when persistent effects or repeated resets are observed.
