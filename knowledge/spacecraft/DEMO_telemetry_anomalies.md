# DEVELOPMENT/DEMO Spacecraft Telemetry Anomalies

## Subsystem monitoring
DEVELOPMENT/DEMO MATERIAL - not real mission documentation. Telemetry anomaly indicators identify measurements outside the detector's learned behavior; they are observations for investigation rather than a diagnosis. Confidence and severity should be considered with the affected channels and mission context.

Operational guidance: review the contributing channels, verify sensor validity, compare against recent telemetry history, and monitor the subsystem before selecting an operational response.
